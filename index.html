Recording of muscle length and action potentials (last 6 seconds)

Step 1 of 6

Tap the patellar tendon

  1. Tendon tap stretches the quadricepsThe tendon runs over the patella, a sesamoid pulley, so a sideways tap becomes a lengthwise stretch of the whole muscle.
  2. Spindle firesStretching the equatorial region is believed to open stretch-sensitive ion channels; the spindle sensory neuron fires action potentials.
  3. Into the cord by the dorsal rootOne synapse: an EPSP on the α motor neuron of the same muscle. A branch excites an inhibitory interneuron.
  4. Out by the ventral rootα spikes reach the quadriceps; the interneuron puts an IPSP on the antagonist’s α motor neuron.
  5. Knee extends, then the loop switches offContraction shortens the spindle, its discharge falls back to the prestretch level: negative feedback.
